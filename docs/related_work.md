# Focused related-work and contribution check

Searched 6 October 2026. This is a focused primary-source check, not a systematic review or evidence of a “first” dataset.

The intended contribution is an auditable linkage of office-address histories to local annual biomass summaries, including the sensitivity of company-year measurement to spatial support and mask choices. Existing corporate nature-risk research already includes textual and spatial measures. The project should differentiate the measurement object and reproducibility evidence without claiming that company-level environmental matching is new.

Queries included `"aboveground biomass" "listed companies"`, `"listed companies" "biomass" biodiversity risk satellite`, `"biodiversity" "Chinese listed" "geographic" firms biomass`, and corporate biodiversity/geolocation variants. They surfaced nearby disclosure and nature-reserve-location studies, but did not establish a comprehensive absence of other AGB linkages. Broader bibliographic and repository coverage is needed before stating a novelty claim.

Titles, authors and identifiers were checked against primary publisher/author sources. Crossref/DOI metadata retrieval is recorded in `paper/reference_metadata.json`; successful BibTeX exports are in `paper/references.bib`. Google Scholar indexing could not be confirmed through the available search, so no citation counts or Scholar coverage claims are made. An unavailable API response is not treated as evidence that a paper does not exist.

A defensible gap statement is: **A reusable office-location biomass linkage needs documented temporal assignment, coordinate reference, spatial support, mask semantics and technical validation before researchers can interpret its company-year values consistently.** The manuscript must establish the achieved contribution through its own methods and sensitivity evidence rather than an unverified absence-of-literature claim.
